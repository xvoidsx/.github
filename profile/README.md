# xvoidsx

xvoidsx is a small independent collective building open software for a
fun, free, and open web. Two people: Raven builds the programming and
integrations, Ryoko owns the design.

## What we make

- **navi** — our agent-native Linux distro. Debian-based, Wayland-only,
  drenched in nightshadeNeon, and built around a simple idea: a computer
  you own. No telemetry, no ads, no lock-in.
- **navi machines** — small computers that run navi, built on the
  Raspberry Pi. The OS stays free on any machine; the machines fund the
  work and the upstream projects navi relies on.
- **navidroid** — the same spirit for your phone: a minimal,
  distractionless Android launcher, no custom ROM needed.
- **The smallweb and the agent-native toolkit** — our fediverse home
  neighborli, the glyyph Nostr client, the cyberia Matrix client, navi
  radio, and the agents and tools that ship with navi.

Everything we make is open source.

## Open research, local agents

We research local AI on open hardware, in the open. The goal is
intelligence on your own compute — your AI never leaves your machine,
and nobody's hardware is below AI. Our model ladder runs from **koe**
for the weakest hardware, through **alice** for personality, to agentic
**lain** for machines with headroom: open weights, quants validated on
real hardware, and papers published alongside the models they describe.

Your hardware is the datacenter.

## Open web, open models

It's the whole bet: the web and the models should both belong to the
people who use them. An open web means protocols over platforms —
smallweb pages, fediverse and Nostr and Matrix instead of walled
gardens, your files on your machine. Open models mean weights you can
inspect, run, and shape yourself, on hardware you already own, instead
of intelligence rented from a gatekeeper. No permission needed to
publish, to compute, or to think. A computer you own, a web you own,
models you own — that's the future we're building toward.
